About CLANKO100

CLANKO100 is a free browser games site made by clanko. Pick a game, press play, and you're in. Nothing to install and no account to make.

Every game is an original

All 21 games on CLANKO100 were made for this site. That means they load fast, they don't show ads in the middle of a level, and they work on computers, tablets and phones. They're grouped into 13 categories, from racing and horror to puzzles and Minecraft-style building.

Games that need a keyboard still have touch controls, and two-player games like Neon Rally and Four Up work on one shared device.

Your saves stay with you

  • Favourites, recently played games, best scores and game saves are kept in your browser on this device.
  • There are no accounts, no sign-ups and no tracking cookies.
  • Clearing your browser data, or using a private window, starts you fresh.

More games are on the way

Got an idea for a game, or found one that isn't working? Send a message and it'll be looked at.

Quick start

  • Press / on any page to search.
  • Can't decide? Try a random game.
  • Tap the heart on a game to save it to your favourites.
  • Use Fullscreen on a game page for the biggest view.
  • Spot a problem? Use “Report a problem” under any game.
