Before you send a report

  • Refresh the page. Most loading hiccups go away.
  • Click inside the game once so it can hear your keyboard.
  • Try “New tab” on the game page if your browser blocks something.
  • Tell us which device and browser you use. It's added to the report for you.

Please don't include passwords or private details in your message.
