About Neon Defense

Build and upgrade towers to stop 25 waves from reaching your core.

Place towers on free squares beside the path. Blasters fire fast, Frost towers slow everything nearby, Cannons hit groups with splash damage and Lasers lock on and heat up. Earn money from each defeat and wave, upgrade towers twice, and keep enough firepower for the boss waves at 10 and 20.

Free, no download Works on phones and tablets Saves your best score

How to play

  • Click a tower in the side panel, then a free square
  • 1 to 4 to pick a tower, Space for the next wave
  • Click a tower to upgrade or sell it, F to toggle 2x speed
