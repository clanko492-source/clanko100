About Neon Snake

The classic snake game with glowing trails, bonus stars and a wrap-around mode.

Steer the snake to eat pink orbs and grow longer. Every fifth orb drops a gold star worth extra points if you reach it before it fades. Choose Walls for the classic rules or Wrap around to slip through the edges of the board. The snake speeds up as it grows, so plan your turns.

Free, no download Works on phones and tablets Saves your best score

How to play

  • Arrow keys or WASD to turn
  • Swipe or tap beside the snake's head on touch screens
  • P or Esc to pause, M to mute
